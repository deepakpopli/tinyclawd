SubagentStart

a squad for subagents.

when Claude Code starts subagents, mini Clawds march in, one for each, and pass a ball around.

another one joins as a substitute. when the last subagent stops, the squad walks back out.

PermissionRequest

it calls you back.

a permission prompt or a question? the whole screen flashes the project name until you answer, so a session never sits there waiting while you are in another window.

several sessions share one Clawd. whoever needs you wins, then whoever is working. dots in the corner show how many are busy.

Stop

it celebrates.

under 20 seconds of work gets a small win. over 2 minutes gets a goal, a trophy or a bicycle kick.

status line

your 5 hour and weekly limits, on its face.

read from the Claude Code status line. running low and it gets tired, then stoppage time. hit the limit and it tells you when you're back.

laptop audio

music on.

play something on the laptop. the song name scrolls by, and the equalizer bars move to the real laptop audio, not a fake loop.

nothing running

nothing running? it has a coffee.

or it juggles, plays football, stares at the stars and naps. at night it falls asleep faster.

what it does is picked at random with cooldowns, so it rarely does the same thing twice in a row.
